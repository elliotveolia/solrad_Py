1. Smoke Impact Magnitude
   - Local smoke: GHI drops 11-17%, DNI drops 32-42% (Corwin et al.)
   - Transported smoke: GHI <5% (but DNI still sensitive)
   - Peak hour losses: 10-30% (Juliano et al.)

2. Forecasting Error Severity
   - NYISO overpredicted by 50% during smoke (Bartram et al.)
   - Hour-ahead forecasts overpredicted by 10-50% (Juliano et al.)
   - Your project: PJM likely faces similar issues

3. Solution Approach
   - Use HRRR-Smoke data (AOD, DSWRF) → R² = 0.85 (Bartram et al.)
   - Integrate aerosol data into weather models → 50% error reduction (Juliano et al.)
   - Machine learning (HGBM) outperforms traditional models

4. Geographic Relevance
   - NYISO (adjacent to PJM) already experiencing this
   - Transported smoke affects DNI significantly
   - PJM likely affected by smoke from Western fires

5. Secondary Variables
   - Wind speed correlation (Bertoletti et al.)
   - Spectral properties of smoke (Masoom et al.)
   - PM2.5 and AOT correlations
